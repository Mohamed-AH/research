# Claiming Ideas

**The episode's argument in one paragraph.** Products can be copied, but knowledge that has been researched, published and patented cannot. Companies that want to last therefore need *knowledge assets*. That means research centres, papers, patents and the people who can turn them into products. In his view, in Saudi Arabia this happens mostly at large companies like Aramco and SABIC. Salman Butt, who co-founded the e-commerce platform Salla and later KnowledgeX, explains how the pipeline works, why it is hard to fund, and what it looks like when it works. His example is a forecast that helped a merchant sell out his warehouse before Eid.

## About the guest

Salman Butt co-founded [Salla](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/saudi-unicorns-how-makkah-based-salla-is-helping-merchants/478045) in Makkah in 2016 with Nawaf Hariri, and served as its CTO.
