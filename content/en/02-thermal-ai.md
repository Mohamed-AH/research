# 2. Deep tech in practice: reading heat

> "We combined more than one technology, and more than one depth in technology, to create this solution." Salman Butt, [16:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t0UiPQnskk&t=986s)

## What Salman says

- **The startup** ([12:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t0UiPQnskk&t=748s)). He describes a Saudi company he invested in that has won international medical awards. He does not name it on air.
- **The science** ([12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t0UiPQnskk&t=771s)). It builds on medical thermography, the study of body temperature. The literature says that a temperature difference between the capillaries of the left and right soles can be an early sign of diabetes complications ([13:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t0UiPQnskk&t=837s)). Diabetic foot is costly and hard to treat once it develops ([14:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t0UiPQnskk&t=865s)).
- **The trick** ([14:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t0UiPQnskk&t=881s)). Detecting this used to need complex, expensive equipment. Now a small thermal camera attached to a phone takes the image, and AI reads differences the human eye cannot see.
- **The mirror** ([16:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t0UiPQnskk&t=995s)). The same team built a mirror that players stand in front of before a match. It reads the heat of peripheral blood vessels and flags muscles at risk of strain. He says it was used during the FIFA World Cup.

## The idea, explained

This is a textbook deep-tech recipe: **old science + newly cheap hardware + AI**.

- *Old science:* thermography has been studied in medicine for decades.
- *Cheap hardware:* thermal sensors that once cost a fortune now clip onto a phone.
- *AI:* a trained model compares the two feet pixel by pixel and turns a fuzzy colour image into a risk score.

None of the three is new on its own. The value is in combining them and proving it works clinically, which is why it counts as deep tech.

## Context and evidence

The company is likely **Amplifai Health** (Riyadh), which is listed in KnowledgeX's portfolio.

- Its diabetic-foot product **TFScan** segments the sole of the foot and analyses its temperature patterns. In a multicentre study with the Saudi Ministry of Health covering 1,120 people, the company says it can find the top 10% of highest-risk patients ([MENA Startup Digest, Dec 2025](https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=23961)).
- In January 2024 it signed an agreement with KAUST to combine its thermography AI with KAUST's hyperspectral imaging ([KAUST](https://kaust.edu.sa/news/kaust-and-amplifai-health-combine-technologies-for-early-diabetes-detection)).
- Its athlete product **NUR** runs on a thermal smart mirror. It tracks recovery and readiness, and was launched in Miami ([Middle East AI News, July 2026](https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/saudi-based-amplifai-launches-ai)).
